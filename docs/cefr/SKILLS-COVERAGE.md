Eight additional prepared four-part reading portfolios teach every source sentence before whole-text questions, source-anchored notes and revised audience briefs. These are not independent assessments or additional essay projects in the 84-project count. Twelve required table-reading tasks teach headings, rows and source comparison in selected A1–A2 units. Twelve additional short practical forms teach labels and field formats and check details from original and changed prepared sources. These are not additional essay projects or independent proficiency tests. All units include eight contextual vocabulary exercises and eight phrase-preparation exercises, bilingual source guides, reading and listening comprehension checks, spoken production, a three-turn recorded role-play, spoken mediation and a topic-specific writing assignment or links to the three original writing projects. Comprehension checks hold feedback until the end and require a fresh check after incorrect answers. Recorded responses require actual recording, replay and self-review; they are not graded for linguistic proficiency.
