%Nuance and style|C1 grammar: reported mood, -te forms, written style, hedging, register and idiom
@u143|Netiesioginė nuosaka|Reported mood|To report something you only heard, Lithuanian has a special form ending in <b>-ąs</b> or <b>-ę</b>: <b>Jis esąs gydytojas</b> means "he is said to be a doctor". It is common in news and folk tales.|Retell a rumour or a fairy tale using the reported forms: what was said to have happened.
#u143a|It is said that…
Jis esąs gydytojas|He is said to be a doctor|yis eh-SAAS gee-DEE-toh-yahs
Ji sakėsi serganti|She said she was ill|yi sah-KAY-si SER-gahn-ti
Gyvenęs kartą karalius|Once upon a time there lived a king|gee-VEH-nays KAR-taa kah-RAH-lyus
Pasak liudininkų, vagis pabėgęs|According to witnesses, the thief escaped|PAH-sahk lyu-di-nin-KOO, vah-GIS pah-BAY-gays
Kalbama, kad ministras atsistatydinsiąs|It is rumoured the minister will resign|KAHL-bah-mah, kahd mi-NIS-trahs aht-sis-tah-tee-din-SYAAS
#u143b|Reporting what others claim
Jie teigia nieko nežiną|They claim to know nothing|yie TAY-gyah NYEH-koh neh-ZHI-naa
Esą kainos kils|Prices are supposedly going to rise|eh-SAA kai-NOHS KILS
Jis sakosi buvęs Paryžiuje|He says he has been in Paris|yis SAH-koh-si BU-vays pah-REE-zhyu-yeh
Pasakojama, kad čia gyvenusi laumė|It is told that a fairy lived here|pah-sah-KOH-yah-mah, kahd CHYAH gee-VEH-nu-si LAU-may
Neva jis viską žinojęs|Supposedly he knew everything|neh-VAH yis VIS-kaa zhi-NOH-yays
@u144|Būdinys|Doing something in a certain way: -te|The rare <b>-te</b> form intensifies a verb: <b>bėgte bėgo</b> means "ran and ran", <b>tylėte tylėjo</b> "was completely silent". It belongs to vivid storytelling.|Tell a short dramatic story using at least three -te forms.
#u144a|Intensifying
bėgte bėgo|ran and ran|BAYK-teh BAY-goh
tylėte tylėjo|was utterly silent|tee-LAY-teh tee-LAY-yoh
verkte verkė|cried and cried|VERK-teh VER-kay
lyte lijo|it poured and poured|LEE-teh LI-yoh
nešte nešė|carried on and on|NESH-teh NEH-shay
#u144b|In sentences
Vaikai bėgte išbėgo į kiemą|The children dashed out into the yard|vai-KAI BAYK-teh ish-BAY-goh ee KYEH-maa
Jis žiūrėte žiūrėjo į ją|He just stared and stared at her|yis zhyoo-RAY-teh zhyoo-RAY-yoh ee YAA
Ji juokte juokėsi|She laughed and laughed|yi YWOHK-teh ywoh-KAY-si
Žmonės plūste plūdo į aikštę|People poured into the square|ZHMOH-nays PLOOS-teh PLOO-doh ee AIKSH-tyay
Sniegas krite krito visą naktį|Snow kept falling all night|SNYEH-gahs KRI-teh KRI-toh VI-saa NAHK-tee
@u145|Dalyviai rašytinėje kalboje|Participles in written style|In writing, a participle can replace a whole clause: instead of "the report, which was prepared by the ministry" you write <b>ministerijos parengta ataskaita</b>.|Rewrite three sentences from a news article in a shorter, written style using participles.
#u145a|Compressing clauses
ministerijos parengta ataskaita|the report prepared by the ministry|mi-nis-TEH-ri-yohs pah-RENK-tah AH-tahs-kai-tah
vyriausybės priimtas sprendimas|the decision taken by the government|vee-ryau-SEE-bays PRYIM-tahs spren-DI-mahs
tyrimą atlikę mokslininkai|the scientists who carried out the research|tee-RI-maa aht-LI-kay mohks-li-nin-KAI
dalyvavusieji posėdyje|those who took part in the meeting|dah-lee-vah-vu-SYEH-yi poh-say-dee-YEH
pateiktini dokumentai|documents to be submitted|pah-tayk-ti-NI doh-ku-MEN-tai
#u145b|Written style
Atsižvelgiant į tai|Taking this into account|aht-sizh-VEL-gyahnt ee TAI
Remiantis tyrimo duomenimis|Based on the research data|REH-myahn-tis tee-RI-moh dwoh-meh-ni-MIS
Išanalizavus rezultatus, nustatyta|Having analysed the results, it was found|ish-ah-nah-li-ZAH-vus reh-zul-TAH-tus, nus-tah-TEE-tah
Pažymėtina, kad|It should be noted that|pah-zhee-MAY-ti-nah, kahd
Kaip minėta anksčiau|As mentioned earlier|kaip mi-NAY-tah ahnks-CHYAU
@u146|Teksto rišlumas|Holding a long text together|Long texts hold together with signposts: <b>visų pirma</b> (first and foremost), <b>be to</b> and <b>apibendrinant</b>, which you know.|Give a two-minute talk on any topic using at least six signposts from this unit.
#u146a|Signposts
Visų pirma|First and foremost|vi-SOO PIR-mah
Toliau aptarsime|Next we will discuss|toh-LYAU ahp-TAR-si-meh
Kalbant apie|Speaking of|KAHL-bahnt AH-pyeh
Kaip jau minėjau|As I have already mentioned|kaip yau mi-NAY-yau
Grįžtant prie|Returning to|GREEZH-tahnt prye
#u146b|Contrast and conclusion
Nepaisant to, kas išdėstyta|Notwithstanding the above|neh-PAI-sahnt TOH, kahs ish-DAYS-tee-tah
Panašiai ir|In the same way|pah-nah-SHYAI IR
Iš to galima daryti išvadą|From this we can conclude|ish TOH GAH-li-mah dah-REE-ti ISH-vah-daa
Galiausiai verta paminėti|Finally, it is worth mentioning|gah-LYAU-syai VER-tah pah-mi-NAY-ti
Baigdamas norėčiau pabrėžti|In closing I would like to stress|BAIG-dah-mahs noh-RAY-chyau pahb-RAYZH-ti
@u147|Atsargus kalbėjimas|Hedging and nuance|At C1 you soften claims: <b>galbūt</b> you know; add <b>regis</b> (it would appear), <b>ko gero</b> (in all likelihood) and <b>tam tikra prasme</b> (in a certain sense).|Give a careful opinion on a controversial topic, softening every claim.
#u147a|Softening
regis|it would appear|REH-gis
ko gero|in all likelihood|koh geh-ROH
tam tikra prasme|in a certain sense|tahm tik-RAH prahs-MEH
iš dalies sutinku|I partly agree|ish dah-LYES su-tin-KU
nesu visiškai tikras|I’m not entirely sure|neh-SU vi-sish-KAI TIK-rahs
#u147b|Careful statements
Galima būtų teigti, kad|One could argue that|GAH-li-mah BOO-too TAYK-ti, kahd
Atrodytų, kad|It would seem that|aht-ROH-dee-too, kahd
Nenorėčiau apibendrinti, bet|I wouldn’t want to generalise, but|neh-noh-RAY-chyau ah-pi-BEN-drin-ti, bet
Tai priklauso nuo to, kaip žiūrėsi|It depends on how you look at it|tai prik-LAU-soh nwoh TOH, kaip zhyoo-RAY-si
Su tam tikromis išlygomis|With certain reservations|su tahm tik-roh-MIS ish-lee-goh-MIS
@u148|Akademinė ir dalykinė kalba|Academic and professional register|Formal writing has its own phrases: <b>šiame straipsnyje</b> (in this article), <b>tikslas yra</b> (the aim is) and <b>atsižvelgiant</b> you know.|Write or say the opening of a formal report: topic, aim, method and structure.
#u148a|Academic phrases
Šiame straipsnyje nagrinėjama|This article examines|shyah-MEH straips-nee-YEH nah-gri-NAY-yah-mah
Tyrimo tikslas yra|The aim of the research is|tee-RI-moh TIKS-lahs ee-RAH
Taikytas metodas|The method used|TAI-kee-tahs meh-TOH-dahs
Rezultatai rodo|The results show|reh-zul-TAH-tai ROH-doh
Būtina pažymėti|It is necessary to note|BOO-ti-nah pah-zhee-MAY-ti
#u148b|Professional phrases
Remdamiesi aukščiau išdėstytu|Based on the above|rem-DAH-mye-si auksh-CHYAU ish-DAYS-tee-tu
Prašome atsižvelgti į|Please take into account|PRAH-shoh-meh aht-sizh-VELK-ti ee
Informuojame, kad|We hereby inform you that|in-for-MWOH-yah-meh, kahd
Iš anksto dėkojame už bendradarbiavimą|Thank you in advance for your cooperation|ish AHNKS-toh day-KOH-yah-meh uzh ben-drah-dar-BYAH-vi-maa
Su pagarba|Respectfully|su pah-gar-BAH
@u149|Kirčiavimas|Stress patterns|Stress in Lithuanian can move: <b>ranka</b> (hand) is stressed at the end, but <b>ranką</b> at the start. You don’t need to learn the four classes by heart, only to recognise that stress moves.|Read pairs of word forms aloud and notice where the stress jumps.
#u149a|Stress that moves
ranka, ranką|hand, the hand (object)|rahn-KAH, RAHN-kaa
galva, galvą|head, the head (object)|gahl-VAH, GAHL-vaa
žiema, žiemą|winter, in winter|zhye-MAH, ZHYEH-maa
diena, dieną|day, by day|dyeh-NAH, DYEH-naa
upė, upę|river, the river (object)|U-pay, U-pyay
#u149b|Same spelling, different stress
antis, antis|duck, bosom (stress shows which)|AHN-tis, ahn-TIS
kasa, kasa|braid, till (stress shows which)|kah-SAH, KAH-sah
pilis, pilis|castle, pours (stress shows which)|pi-LIS, PI-lis
kartas, kartas|time (once), bitter|KAR-tahs, kar-TAHS
žirnis, žirniai|pea, peas|ZHIR-nis, zhir-NYAI
