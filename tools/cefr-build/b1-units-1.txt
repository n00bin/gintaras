%Telling it fully|B1 grammar: calling people, used to, would, said that, the one who
@u60|Visi linksniai|All the cases|When you call someone, the name changes: <b>Jonai</b> is how you call Jonas, <b>Petrai</b> is how you call Petras. This calling form is the seventh case.|Call three people by name and ask each of them something, then say what you have and don't have, using "no" + the "of" form.

@u61|Ką darydavome|What we used to do|To say "used to", put <b>-dav-</b> into the past: <b>žaisdavau</b> is "I used to play". It is for things that happened again and again.|Tell what you used to do as a child: in summer, in winter, and at your grandparents’.
#u61a|Used to: -davo

@u62|Jei galėčiau|If I could|"Would" is one ending: <b>galėčiau</b> is "I could", <b>būtų</b> is "it would be". Put <b>jei</b> in front and you have "if".|Say three things you would do if you had more time, and ask someone politely for help with "Ar galėtum…?"
#u62a|Would and could

Ką darytum mano vietoje?|What would you do in my place?|kaa dah-REE-tum MAH-noh VYEH-toh-yeh
Aš to nedaryčiau|I wouldn’t do that|ahsh toh neh-dah-REE-chyau
#u62b|If it happened

@u64|Tas, kuris|The one: -asis, -oji|Add <b>-is</b> or <b>-ji</b> to a describing word and it points at one thing: <b>naujasis</b> is "the new one". You see it in names: <b>Naujieji metai</b> is New Year.|Point at things around you and pick one: the new one, the big one, the red one.
