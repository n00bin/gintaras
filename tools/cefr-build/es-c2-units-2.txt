However, the aim wasn’t to generalise, but to understand in depth how those affected experience the phenomenon.

Así lo haré. Reformularé las conclusiones para dejar claro el alcance del estudio y añadiré una sección sobre sus limitaciones.|I will. I’ll reword the conclusions to make the scope of the study clear and add a section on its limitations.

No es tanto el precio como el volumen. Our storage capacity is limited, and exclusivity would force us to give up other suppliers we have worked with for years.
Y lo hay, sin duda.
