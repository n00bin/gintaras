@u96|Dalyviai|Participles in full|A participle is a verb used as a describing word. <b>skaitoma knyga</b> is "a book being read", <b>perskaityta knyga</b> "a book that has been read".|Describe things around you with participles: something being built, something already finished, something that will be done.
