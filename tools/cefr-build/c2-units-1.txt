@u187|Senosios ir literatūrinės formos|Old and literary forms|Old books and folk songs keep forms modern speech has lost. The dual number talks about exactly two: <b>mudu</b> (we two) and <b>Mudu einava</b> (we two are going). Stories also use literary verbs like <b>tarė</b> (said).|Retell a short folk tale in a slightly old-fashioned style, with at least one dual form and two literary verbs.

@u190|Tikslus perfrazavimas|Precise paraphrase and mediation|At C2 you pass on what sources say, accurately and in your own words. <b>Tiksliau sakant</b> (to be precise) narrows a claim, and <b>Pasak antrojo šaltinio</b> (according to the second source) reports one.|Read two short texts on the same topic and explain to someone what each says and where they disagree.
