%B1 · How Spanish works, part 3|Telling stories, perfect and future, the subjunctive, pronouns and reported speech
@u89|Contar historias|Telling stories: preterite and imperfect together|In a story, the imperfect sets the scene (<b>Hacía frío</b>, it was cold) and the preterite moves the action on (<b>De repente sonó el teléfono</b>, suddenly the phone rang).|Tell the story of something unexpected that happened to you, setting the scene first.
#u89a|Setting the scene, then the action
Hacía frío y estaba lloviendo.|It was cold and it was raining.
Eran las once de la noche cuando sonó el teléfono.|It was eleven at night when the phone rang.
De repente sonó el teléfono.|Suddenly the phone rang.
Mientras cocinaba, se fue la luz.|While I was cooking, the power went off.
Estábamos en la playa cuando empezó la tormenta.|We were at the beach when the storm started.
Yo dormía tranquilamente cuando alguien tocó la puerta.|I was sleeping peacefully when someone knocked at the door.
Había mucha gente, así que nos fuimos a otro lugar.|There were lots of people, so we went somewhere else.
No sabía qué hacer, entonces le llamé a mi hermano.|I didn't know what to do, so I called my brother.
#u89b|Story words
Al principio todo iba bien.|At first everything was going well.
Un día, sin avisar, apareció en mi puerta.|One day, without warning, he turned up at my door.
Al final, todo salió bien.|In the end, everything turned out fine.
Mientras tanto, mi esposa esperaba en el carro.|Meanwhile, my wife was waiting in the car.
Unos minutos después llegó la policía.|A few minutes later the police arrived.
@u90|Lo que has hecho|What you have done: the present perfect|<b>He vivido aquí</b> (I have lived here) and <b>Nunca he visto</b> (I have never seen) use a form of "have" plus the -ado / -ido form. In Mexico people often use the simple past instead, as the second part shows.|Talk about three things you have never done and three you have already done this year.
#u90a|Have done, have never done
He vivido aquí toda mi vida.|I have lived here all my life.
¿Has estado en Oaxaca?|Have you been to Oaxaca?
Nunca he visto la nieve.|I have never seen snow.
Mis papás han viajado mucho.|My parents have travelled a lot.
Este año hemos ahorrado poco.|This year we have saved little.
Todavía no he terminado el informe.|I haven't finished the report yet.
¿Alguna vez has comido chapulines?|Have you ever eaten grasshoppers?
#u90b|The Mexican way: ya and todavía
¿Ya comiste?|Have you eaten yet?
Ya terminé, gracias.|I've already finished, thanks.
¿Ya llegaron tus primos?|Have your cousins arrived yet?
Todavía no llegan.|They haven't arrived yet.
Ya vi esa película dos veces.|I've already seen that film twice.
@u91|Lo que pasará y lo que pasaría|What will happen and what would happen|The future adds endings to the whole verb: <b>viviré</b> (I will live), <b>lloverá</b> (it will rain). The second part adds "would", which works the same way.|Say what you will do next year, and what you would do with a lot of money.
#u91a|The future
Mañana lloverá en todo el país.|Tomorrow it will rain across the whole country.
El próximo año viviré en Monterrey.|Next year I will live in Monterrey.
¿Vendrás a la boda?|Will you come to the wedding?
Te lo diré cuando lo sepa.|I'll tell you when I know.
Seguramente estará en su oficina.|He'll probably be in his office.
¿Qué hora será?|I wonder what time it is.
#u91b|The conditional
Me gustaría vivir junto al mar.|I would like to live by the sea.
¿Podrías ayudarme con esto?|Could you help me with this?
Yo en tu lugar no lo compraría.|If I were you I wouldn't buy it.
Con más tiempo, aprendería a tocar el piano.|With more time, I would learn to play the piano.
Dijo que llegaría temprano.|He said he would arrive early.
Deberías descansar un poco.|You should rest a little.
@u92|Ojalá: el subjuntivo|Wishes, advice and feelings: the present subjunctive|After words of wishing, advising and feeling, the verb changes: <b>Quiero que vengas</b> (I want you to come), <b>Ojalá que llueva</b> (I hope it rains).|Give a friend advice for a job interview, starting with "Te recomiendo que…".
#u92a|Wishes and advice
Quiero que vengas a mi fiesta.|I want you to come to my party.
Ojalá que no llueva mañana.|I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow.
Te recomiendo que pruebes el mole.|I recommend that you try the mole.
Es mejor que salgamos temprano.|It's better that we leave early.
Mi mamá quiere que estudie medicina.|My mum wants me to study medicine.
El doctor dice que descanse más.|The doctor says I should rest more.
Espero que te guste el regalo.|I hope you like the present.
#u92b|Feelings and doubt
Me alegra que estés aquí.|I'm glad you're here.
Me molesta que lleguen tarde.|It bothers me that they arrive late.
Siento mucho que estés enfermo.|I'm very sorry you're ill.
No creo que sea tan difícil.|I don't think it's that difficult.
Dudo que tengan mesa a esta hora.|I doubt they have a table at this time.
Es posible que cambien el horario.|They may change the timetable.
Cuando llegues, mándame un mensaje.|When you arrive, send me a message.
@u93|No lo hagas|Don't do it: negative commands and two pronouns together|Telling someone not to do something uses the subjunctive: <b>No vengas</b> (don't come), <b>No llegues tarde</b> (don't be late). With two pronouns, le becomes <b>se</b>: <b>Se lo doy</b> (I give it to him).|Give a younger relative five pieces of advice about what not to do.
#u93a|Don't
No vengas mañana.|Don't come tomorrow.
No llegues tarde.|Don't be late.
No te preocupes por eso.|Don't worry about that.
No toques eso, está caliente.|Don't touch that, it's hot.
No le digas a nadie.|Don't tell anyone.
No se olviden de sus boletos.|Don't forget your tickets, all of you.
No hagas ruido, el bebé está dormido.|Don't make noise, the baby is asleep.
#u93b|Two pronouns together
Se lo doy mañana.|I'll give it to him tomorrow.
¿Me lo prestas?|Will you lend it to me?
Te lo mando por correo.|I'll send it to you by email.
Ya se lo dije a tu mamá.|I already told your mum.
Dámelo, por favor.|Give it to me, please.
No se lo digas a nadie.|Don't tell anyone about it.
@u94|Que, quien, donde, lo que|Linking words: que, quien, donde, lo que|Join ideas with <b>que</b> (that, who, which), <b>donde</b> (where) and <b>lo que</b> (what, the thing that): <b>La casa donde nací</b> (the house where I was born).|Describe a person, a place and an object that are important to you, using que, donde and lo que.
#u94a|That, who, where, what
El libro que me prestaste es muy bueno.|The book you lent me is very good.
La señora que vive arriba es doctora.|The woman who lives upstairs is a doctor.
Esta es la casa donde nací.|This is the house where I was born.
El amigo con quien viajé se llama Rafa.|The friend I travelled with is called Rafa.
Lo que más me gusta es la comida.|What I like most is the food.
No entiendo lo que dices.|I don't understand what you're saying.
Haz lo que quieras.|Do whatever you want.
El restaurante del que te hablé cerró.|The restaurant I told you about closed.
@u95|Lo que dijo|What they said: reported speech, and dates|To pass on what someone said: <b>Dijo que estaba cansado</b> (he said he was tired), <b>Me preguntó si quería café</b> (she asked me if I wanted coffee).|Tell a friend about a phone call you had, reporting what the other person said.
#u95a|Passing on what someone said
Dijo que estaba cansado.|He said he was tired.
Me dijo que no podía venir.|She told me she couldn't come.
Me preguntó si quería un café.|She asked me if I wanted a coffee.
Dicen que va a llover toda la semana.|They say it's going to rain all week.
Le pregunté dónde vivía.|I asked him where he lived.
Me contó que había perdido su trabajo.|He told me he had lost his job.
Me pidió que le ayudara.|She asked me to help her.
#u95b|First, second, third: dates and order
Vivo en el tercer piso.|I live on the third floor.
Es la primera vez que vengo.|It's the first time I've come.
Llegó en segundo lugar.|She came second.
Es su cuarto hijo.|It's their fourth child.
Nació el primero de mayo de dos mil diez.|He was born on the first of May two thousand and ten.
Estamos en el siglo veintiuno.|We are in the twenty-first century.
