@u320|Si así no lo hiciere…|Old forms you will meet in writing: the future subjunctive and the past anterior|Legal texts and old literature use forms that are rare in speech, like the future subjunctive: <b>El que infringiere esta ley será sancionado</b> (whoever breaks this law will be punished), and the past anterior: <b>Apenas hubo terminado, se marchó</b> (as soon as he had finished, he left). You only need to recognise them.|Read a short legal sentence aloud and explain what it means in everyday Spanish.
#u320a|The future subjunctive in legal language
El que infringiere esta ley será sancionado.|Whoever breaks this law will be punished.
Apenas hubo terminado, se marchó.|As soon as he had finished, he left.
Si así no lo hiciere, que la Nación me lo demande.|And if I should not do so, may the Nation hold me to account.
Quien no compareciere en el plazo señalado perderá su derecho.|Whoever fails to appear within the stated time limit will lose their right.
Lo que el contrato estipulare prevalecerá sobre cualquier acuerdo verbal.|Whatever the contract may stipulate will prevail over any verbal agreement.
Adonde fueres, haz lo que vieres.|When in Rome, do as the Romans do.
#u320b|The past anterior in literature
Una vez que hubo firmado, se levantó de la mesa.|Once he had signed, he got up from the table.
Luego que hubieron cenado, salieron al jardín.|As soon as they had dined, they went out into the garden.
No bien hubo amanecido, partieron hacia la sierra.|No sooner had day broken than they set off for the mountains.
Cuando hubo leído la carta, rompió a llorar.|When she had read the letter, she burst into tears.
@u321|Eso sí que no|Word order for emphasis and style|Spanish moves words to the front to stress them: <b>Eso sí que no lo acepto</b> (that I will not accept), <b>Lo que más me molesta es su actitud</b> (what bothers me most is his attitude).|Say three things that annoy you, putting the most important part at the beginning of each sentence.
#u321a|Putting the key idea first
Eso sí que no lo acepto.|That I will not accept.
Lo que más me molesta es su actitud.|What bothers me most is his attitude.
A ese hombre no lo vuelvo a invitar.|That man I won’t invite again.
Fue ella quien descubrió el error.|It was she who found the mistake.
Dinero no tenemos, pero ganas sí.|Money we don’t have, but enthusiasm we do.
Donde mejor se come es en casa de mi abuela.|Where you eat best is at my grandmother’s house.
#u321b|Inversion and rhythm
Grande fue nuestra sorpresa al ver el resultado.|Great was our surprise when we saw the result.
Mucho me temo que no llegaremos a tiempo.|I very much fear we won’t arrive in time.
Bien sabes que no fue mi intención.|You know full well it wasn’t my intention.
Ni una palabra dijo en toda la cena.|Not a word did he say during the whole dinner.
@u322|¡Qué bonito!|Irony, wordplay and double meanings|Irony often says the opposite of what it means, with the tone doing the work: <b>¡Qué puntualidad!</b> (what punctuality! — to someone very late), <b>Vaya, qué suerte la mía</b> (well, just my luck).|Tell a short anecdote using irony, then explain the joke to someone who missed it.
#u322a|Saying the opposite
¡Qué puntualidad!|What punctuality!|Said ironically to someone who arrives very late.
Vaya, qué suerte la mía.|Well, just my luck.
¡Lo que nos faltaba!|That’s all we needed!
Sí, claro, y yo soy el rey de Inglaterra.|Yes, sure, and I’m the King of England.
Muy bonito: ni siquiera avisaste.|Very nice: you didn’t even let anyone know.
Genial, otra junta que pudo haber sido un correo.|Great, another meeting that could have been an email.
#u322b|Playing with words
El que es perico donde quiera es verde.|Talent shows wherever you are.|Literally: a parrot is green wherever it is.
Me salió el tiro por la culata.|It backfired on me.|Literally: the shot came out of the butt of the gun.
Le dieron atole con el dedo.|They strung him along with empty promises.
Se le cayó el veinte.|The penny finally dropped.|Mexican: literally “the twenty-cent coin dropped”, from old payphones.
@u323|Dicho de otra forma|Paraphrase and mediation|To pass on someone else’s ideas or simplify them, use set phrases: <b>Lo que quiere decir es que…</b> (what he means is that…), <b>En pocas palabras, no hay presupuesto</b> (in a nutshell, there is no budget).|Explain to a friend, in simple words, a complicated notice or rule you have read recently.
#u323a|Rephrasing and summarising
Lo que quiere decir es que no está de acuerdo.|What he means is that he doesn’t agree.
En pocas palabras, no hay presupuesto.|In a nutshell, there is no budget.
Dicho de otra forma, tendremos que esperar.|Put another way, we will have to wait.
Si lo entendí bien, la reunión se pospone.|If I understood correctly, the meeting is postponed.
O sea, que no vienen hasta mañana.|So, they aren’t coming until tomorrow.
Resumiendo, ambas partes quieren lo mismo.|To sum up, both sides want the same thing.
#u323b|Passing on positions fairly
Según entiendo, a ustedes les preocupa el costo.|As I understand it, you are worried about the cost.
Por lo que dice, el problema no es el horario sino el trato.|From what she says, the problem isn’t the hours but the way she is treated.
Me parece que ambos tienen parte de razón.|I think you both have a point.
Permítanme aclarar lo que propone la señora.|Allow me to clarify what the lady is proposing.
@u324|Se hace constar|Legal, academic and administrative language|Official texts have fixed formulas: <b>Por medio del presente se notifica…</b> (you are hereby notified…), <b>Se hace constar que el interesado…</b> (it is hereby certified that the applicant…).|Read out a formal notice, then say what it means in everyday words.
#u324a|Administrative formulas
Por medio del presente se notifica al interesado.|The applicant is hereby notified.
Se hace constar que el interesado cursó sus estudios en esta institución.|It is hereby certified that the applicant studied at this institution.
El abajo firmante declara bajo protesta de decir verdad.|The undersigned declares under oath to tell the truth.
Sin otro particular, reciba un cordial saludo.|Without further ado, please accept my kind regards.
Para los fines legales a que haya lugar.|For whatever legal purposes may apply.
Favor de presentarse con identificación oficial vigente.|Please attend with valid official identification.
#u324b|Legal and academic style
En virtud de lo dispuesto en el artículo cuarto, se niega la solicitud.|Pursuant to the provisions of article four, the application is refused.
El presente estudio tiene por objeto analizar la migración interna.|The aim of this study is to analyse internal migration.
Los resultados obtenidos permiten afirmar que la hipótesis es correcta.|The results obtained allow us to state that the hypothesis is correct.
Queda prohibida la reproducción total o parcial de esta obra.|Total or partial reproduction of this work is prohibited.
@u325|Más vale tarde que nunca|Sayings and proverbs|Sayings sum up popular wisdom in a few words: <b>Más vale tarde que nunca</b> (better late than never), <b>Camarón que se duerme se lo lleva la corriente</b> (if you snooze, you lose).|Choose three sayings and tell a short story where each one fits.
#u325a|Everyday sayings
Más vale tarde que nunca.|Better late than never.
Camarón que se duerme se lo lleva la corriente.|If you snooze, you lose.|Literally: the shrimp that falls asleep is carried away by the current.
Al que madruga, Dios lo ayuda.|The early bird catches the worm.
No hay mal que por bien no venga.|Every cloud has a silver lining.
Dime con quién andas y te diré quién eres.|You are judged by the company you keep.
En casa del herrero, cuchillo de palo.|The shoemaker’s children go barefoot.|Literally: in the blacksmith’s house, a wooden knife.
#u325b|Wise and wry sayings
Más vale pájaro en mano que ciento volando.|A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.
A caballo regalado no se le ve el colmillo.|Don’t look a gift horse in the mouth.
El que mucho abarca poco aprieta.|Don’t bite off more than you can chew.
Ojos que no ven, corazón que no siente.|What the eye doesn’t see, the heart doesn’t grieve over.
Del dicho al hecho hay mucho trecho.|Easier said than done.
Cría fama y échate a dormir.|Once you have a reputation, it follows you.
