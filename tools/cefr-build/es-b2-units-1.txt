@u149|Cuando, para que, aunque|When, so that, although: the subjunctive after linking words|About the future or a purpose, these words take the subjunctive: <b>Cuando llegues, avísame</b> (when you arrive, let me know), <b>Te lo explico para que entiendas</b> (I'll explain so that you understand).|Explain your plans for next year using cuando, en cuanto and antes de que.
#u149a|When and as soon as
Cuando llegues, avísame.|When you arrive, let me know.
Te lo explico para que entiendas.|I'll explain it so that you understand.
En cuanto termine, te llamo.|As soon as I finish, I'll call you.
Hasta que no me pagues, no te ayudo.|Until you pay me, I won't help you.
Vamos a salir antes de que empiece a llover.|We're going to leave before it starts to rain.
Cuando sea grande, quiero ser piloto.|When I grow up, I want to be a pilot.
#u149b|So that, although, without
Habla más fuerte para que todos te oigan.|Speak louder so that everyone can hear you.
Aunque llueva, vamos a ir a la playa.|Even if it rains, we're going to the beach.
Aunque llueve, salgo a correr.|Although it's raining, I'm going out running.
Salió sin que nadie lo viera.|He left without anyone seeing him.
Te presto el carro con tal de que me lo devuelvas lleno.|I'll lend you the car as long as you bring it back full.
En caso de que no puedas venir, mándame un mensaje.|In case you can't come, send me a message.
@u150|Quisiera que fuera así|The imperfect subjunctive|When the main verb is in the past or the conditional, the subjunctive moves to the past too: <b>Me pidió que viniera</b> (she asked me to come), <b>Quería que fuera</b> (he wanted me to go).|Tell someone what your parents wanted you to do when you were a child.
#u150a|Wanted, asked, hoped
Me pidió que viniera temprano.|She asked me to come early.
Mi papá quería que fuera ingeniero.|My dad wanted me to be an engineer.
Les dije que no hicieran ruido.|I told them not to make noise.
Esperaba que me llamaras.|I was hoping you'd call me.
Era necesario que todos estuvieran de acuerdo.|It was necessary for everyone to agree.
No creía que fuera posible.|I didn't believe it was possible.
#u150b|Polite and hypothetical
Quisiera hacer una reservación.|I would like to make a booking.
Ojalá tuviera más tiempo.|I wish I had more time.
Me habló como si fuéramos amigos de toda la vida.|He spoke to me as if we were lifelong friends.
Si pudieras cambiar algo, ¿qué cambiarías?|If you could change something, what would you change?
@u151|Si tuviera…|If I had…: hypothetical if-clauses|For things that aren't true now, <b>si</b> + imperfect subjunctive goes with the conditional: <b>Si tuviera dinero, viajaría</b> (if I had money, I'd travel).|Say three things you would do if you won the lottery, and three you would never do.
#u151a|If I had, I would
Si tuviera dinero, viajaría por todo el mundo.|If I had money, I would travel all over the world.
Si fuera tú, aceptaría el trabajo.|If I were you, I'd accept the job.
Si viviera cerca del mar, nadaría todos los días.|If I lived near the sea, I would swim every day.
Si supiera la respuesta, te la diría.|If I knew the answer, I'd tell you.
Si no lloviera tanto, saldríamos a caminar.|If it didn't rain so much, we'd go out for a walk.
¿Qué harías si perdieras tu trabajo?|What would you do if you lost your job?
Si tuviéramos un jardín, tendríamos un perro.|If we had a garden, we would have a dog.
@u152|Ya había pasado|It had already happened: the pluperfect|For something earlier than another past event: <b>Cuando llegué, ya se había ido</b> (when I arrived, she had already left).|Tell a story where you arrived too late for something.
#u152a|Had done
Cuando llegué a la estación, el tren ya se había ido.|When I got to the station, the train had already left.
Nunca había visto tanta gente junta.|I had never seen so many people together.
Me di cuenta de que había olvidado la cartera.|I realised I had forgotten my wallet.
Ya habíamos cenado cuando nos invitaron.|We had already had dinner when they invited us.
Me dijo que nunca había salido de su pueblo.|He told me he had never left his village.
Para entonces, ya habían cerrado la tienda.|By then, they had already closed the shop.
@u153|Se vende, fue construido|The passive and impersonal se|Spanish often uses <b>se</b> when the doer doesn't matter: <b>Se venden departamentos</b> (flats for sale), <b>Se dice que…</b> (people say that…). With <b>ser</b> it's more formal, as the second part shows.|Describe how things are done in your town: where things are sold, what is eaten, what people say.
#u153a|Se: when the doer doesn't matter
Se venden departamentos en esta zona.|Flats are for sale in this area.
Se habla español e inglés.|Spanish and English spoken.
Se dice que va a subir la gasolina.|People say petrol is going to go up.
En México se come mucho maíz.|In Mexico people eat a lot of corn.
Aquí no se permite fumar.|Smoking isn't allowed here.
Se busca persona con experiencia.|Experienced person wanted.
#u153b|The passive with ser
El puente fue construido en mil novecientos diez.|The bridge was built in nineteen ten.
La ley fue aprobada por el Congreso.|The law was passed by Congress.
Los ganadores serán anunciados mañana.|The winners will be announced tomorrow.
El ladrón fue detenido en la frontera.|The thief was arrested at the border.
La novela fue traducida a veinte idiomas.|The novel was translated into twenty languages.
@u154|Habré terminado, habría ido|Will have done, would have done|<b>Habré terminado</b> means "I will have finished"; <b>habría ido</b> means "I would have gone". Mexicans also use the first for guessing: <b>¿Qué habrá pasado?</b> (I wonder what happened).|Talk about something you would have done differently in the past.
#u154a|Will have, would have, I wonder
Para el viernes habré terminado el proyecto.|By Friday I will have finished the project.
Yo en tu lugar no habría dicho nada.|In your place I wouldn't have said anything.
Si hubiera sabido, habría venido antes.|If I had known, I would have come earlier.
¿Qué habrá pasado con Luis?|I wonder what happened to Luis.
Ya habrán llegado a Puebla.|They'll have arrived in Puebla by now.
Me habría gustado conocer a mi abuelo.|I would have liked to meet my grandfather.
@u155|Formal e informal|Formal and informal Spanish|The same idea sounds very different in a formal letter and with friends: <b>Le agradezco su atención</b> (I thank you for your attention) and <b>Gracias, eres un sol</b> (thanks, you're a star).|Write the same request twice: once to a company and once to a close friend.
#u155a|Formal
Le agradezco su atención.|I thank you for your attention.
Por medio de la presente le informo que su solicitud fue aceptada.|I hereby inform you that your application has been accepted.
Le ruego que me envíe la documentación.|I kindly ask you to send me the documents.
Sin otro particular, le envío un cordial saludo.|With nothing further, I send you my kind regards.
Quedo a sus órdenes para cualquier aclaración.|I remain at your disposal for any clarification.
#u155b|Informal
Gracias, eres un sol.|Thanks, you're a star.
Oye, ¿me echas la mano con algo?|Hey, can you give me a hand with something?
No te agüites, todo va a salir bien.|Don't be down, it'll all turn out fine.
¿Nos vemos al rato?|See you in a bit?
Ahí nos vidrios.|See you around. (slang)
@u156|Dichos y expresiones|Idioms and expressions|Idioms say a lot in a few words: <b>echarle ganas</b> (to give it your all), <b>Metí la pata</b> (I put my foot in it).|Use three of these expressions to describe a week where everything went wrong.
#u156a|Everyday idioms
Hay que echarle ganas.|You have to give it your all.
Metí la pata con mi jefe.|I put my foot in it with my boss.
Ese examen fue pan comido.|That exam was a piece of cake.
Estás en las nubes.|You're daydreaming.
Ponte las pilas, que ya casi es el examen.|Get your act together, the exam is almost here.
Ese carro le costó un ojo de la cara.|That car cost him an arm and a leg.
#u156b|More Mexican expressions
Me dio el avión toda la plática.|He ignored me the whole conversation.
Ya estoy hasta el gorro de tanto tráfico.|I'm fed up with so much traffic.
Me hice bolas con las instrucciones.|I got muddled with the instructions.
No le busques tres pies al gato.|Don't overcomplicate things.
Es muy directa, no tiene pelos en la lengua.|She's very direct, she doesn't mince her words.
La película me sacó de onda.|The film threw me.
