Face-to-face translator

Talk with anyone

Put your phone on the table between you. Each person gets their own half of the screen, the far half upside down so it faces them. Tap the microphone, speak, and the other person sees and hears it in their language.

Good to know

Your phone listens in the language set for each side. If someone types, or says something in another language, Gintaras spots it and switches that side to the right language. It needs an internet connection, and what you say is sent to the translation service to be translated (see Privacy).

App

Install as an app

Put Gintaras on your home screen like any other app: its own icon, full screen, no browser bars, and it opens even without signal. It also keeps your saved lessons safe for offline. Your progress stays exactly where it is.

Use it offline

Gintaras keeps learning without signal. While you are online it saves what comes next by itself: the rest of this unit and the next two, their conversations, and the words due this week, with their sound and pictures. Going away for longer? Download the whole course.

Labas! Let's learn Lithuanian.

Never learned a language before? Bad at it? Good. This app assumes both. You start with hello and thank you, and by the end you can hold a real conversation.

1One word at a time. Every word comes with a picture where it can, a plain English "sounds like", and a real Lithuanian voice. You pick a new word from choices once, then spell it, type it and say it yourself, because that is what makes it stick.
2Stuck? Say so. Every question has an "I don't know" button. No guessing, no shame: you see the answer and the word comes back a moment later.
3Words come back. The app reminds you of a word right before you would forget it, then waits longer each time. That is the whole trick to remembering.
4Then you talk. Every few units there is a real conversation using only words you know. You write each reply, then say it out loud.
