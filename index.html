Face-to-face translator

Talk with anyone

Put your phone on the table between you. Each person gets their own half of the screen, the far half upside down so it faces them. Tap the microphone, speak, and the other person sees and hears it in their language.

Good to know

Your phone listens in the language set for each side. If someone types, or says something in another language, Gintaras spots it and switches that side to the right language. It needs an internet connection, and what you say is sent to the translation service to be translated (see Privacy).

Labas! Let's learn Lithuanian.

Never learned a language before? Bad at it? Good. This app assumes both. You start with sounds, and by the end you can hold a real conversation.

1One word at a time. Every word comes with a picture where it can, a plain English "sounds like", and a real Lithuanian voice. Tap, listen, match. You only type a word once you already know it.
2Stuck? Say so. Every question has an "I don't know" button. No guessing, no shame: you see the answer and the word comes back a moment later.
3Words come back. The app reminds you of a word right before you would forget it, then waits longer each time. That is the whole trick to remembering.
4Then you talk. Every few units there is a real conversation using only words you know. You write each reply, then say it out loud.
How much time each day?

Every word is spoken by a Lithuanian voice. Tap the speaker on any word to hear it.

Make it about you

The first thing people ask is your name and where you're from. Tell me now and the lessons use your answers, so you practise what you'll really say. Skip anything you like.

Some words change for men and women. Which fits you?

Want the lessons even more about you? Add your work, family and hobbies any time on your Profile, under About me.

Save your progress

Sign in and everything you learn is saved to your account from the very first word: your lessons, streak and XP, on any phone or computer. You also go straight onto the leaderboard.
