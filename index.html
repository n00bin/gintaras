Reading "sounds like"

Plain English spelling, split into syllables. The bolder syllable is the one you stress. Each spelling below always means the same sound.

ahas in father
e, ehas in bet
ayas in day
ias in bit
eeas in see
ohas in go
uas in put
ooas in moon
owas in cow
aias in aisle, the "eye" sound (TAIP sounds like "type")
yalways as in yes: yah, yeh, yoh, yu
zhas in measure
tsas in cats

Lithuanian letters

Lithuanian is written the way it sounds. Learn these nine and you can read almost anything.

ą ę į ųlong vowels (the hook used to mean a nasal sound; today it only marks length): ačiū, žąsis
ėa closed "e", like the vowel in "they" without the glide: tėtis, mėsa
ū, ylong "oo" and long "ee": sūris, vynas
č š ž"ch", "sh", and the "s" in "measure": čia, šuo, žalias
calways "ts": cukrus
jlike English "y": jūra, ji
rrolled, as in Spanish: rytas

About this course

Progress is saved on this device in your browser. Clearing site data or switching devices starts fresh. Audio is a Lithuanian neural voice (Ona or Leonas); the device's own voice is used only if a clip fails to load.

Privacy: what is stored and how to delete it

This erases your XP, streak and every lesson. There is no undo.

Labas! Let's learn Lithuanian.

Never learned a language before? Bad at it? Good. This app assumes both. You start with sounds, and by the end you can hold a real conversation.

1One word at a time. Every word comes with a picture where it can, a plain English "sounds like", and a real Lithuanian voice. Tap, listen, match. You only type a word once you already know it.
2Stuck? Say so. Every question has an "I don't know" button. No guessing, no shame: you see the answer and the word comes back a moment later.
3Words come back. The app reminds you of a word right before you would forget it, then waits longer each time. That is the whole trick to remembering.
4Then you talk. Every few units there is a real conversation using only words you know. Text it like a chat, or say it out loud.
How much time each day?

Every word is spoken by a Lithuanian voice. Tap the speaker on any word to hear it.
