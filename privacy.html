Privacy

Gintaras is a free hobby app for learning Lithuanian. Last updated 27 October 2026 (account deletion and leagues).

Without an account

Everything stays on your device. Your lessons, words, streak and settings are saved in your browser, and the recordings you download for offline use are saved there too. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account (optional)

If you create an account so your progress follows you between devices and you appear on the leaderboard, these are stored with our database provider, Supabase:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • Your nickname, XP, streak, level and weekly league. These are shown publicly in the League tab. Your nickname starts as your first name (from Google) or the start of your email address, and you can change it any time on Profile.
  • Your learning progress (which lessons and words you have done), visible only to you.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and basic profile (name and picture) so we can create your account. We do not get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

What we never do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We never email you, except an email you ask for, like a password reset.

Microphone

In speaking lessons and conversations you can tap the microphone to check your pronunciation. Your browser does the listening: Google's speech service on Android and in Chrome, Apple's on iPhone. Gintaras only receives the words that were heard, uses them for that one check, and does not keep them or any audio.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself, any time: open Settings (the ⚙ at the top right), go to Account, and tap Delete my account. It happens straight away and removes your account, your nickname, your place in the league, and the progress saved online. There is no undo.

Can't sign in any more? Email n00binhard@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and it will be deleted for you.

To clear what is saved on a phone or computer, use Settings → About → Reset all progress, or clear the site's data in your browser.
