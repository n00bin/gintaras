# Gintaras version history

How the numbers work: the **first** number goes up for a big change, the **middle** number for a new feature, the **last** number for a fix or polish.

The app shows its version on the opening screen and at the bottom of Settings (`APP_RELEASE` in index.html).

- **3.91** (2026-11-02) Round U (judges' round 7). The missing New word cards: "Move on by itself" moved a right answer on to the next card, and a tap meant for the result landed on that card's Got it, so a new word went by unseen (Ačiū, Laba diena, Malonu susipažinti, Iki pasimatymo for both judges). It now stops before a New word card, a tip, a speaking card and the end of a lesson, and works out what really comes next first. tools/check_cards.py plays with Move on by itself on and presses Continue a moment late, like a person reading the result (the old version failed it 197 times). Choices on a sentence card are only words you've met (Labą is gone from Unit 1; 7 drills with no met choice are off); praise is Correct! until Gerai or Puiku is taught; the checker also reads results and any word with a Lithuanian letter. Conversations: every word they use that you haven't met is underlined with its meaning (226 meanings added, names said to you too: Markai), listed before the chat starts, and their lines stay in Lithuanian (a reply that repeats theirs isn't counted as remembered). Speak it without a microphone: I said it out loud is the big button on the result, speaking cards never move on by themselves, no XP for one not said, and typed with speaking paused opens the next step (one sentence says so). One judge: conversations use the lessons' judge (your name without -as is Nearly, Man irgi for Aš irgi is Nearly with the rule), Čia esame and Iš Amerikos esu are right with the usual order, irgi and ne keep their place (Man gerai irgi isn't right), Dar kartą, prašau works for asking to repeat, O tu? isn't a wrong choice any more, Say good day and no thanks needs both in Quick chat too, a second miss shows the line. Notes: one letter off inside a word (tokjo) is Nearly; ne for Yes says ne means no; an order that isn't right says which word has its place; a part of a reply says what's still missing; letter tiles ignore spaces. Say it without a microphone keeps the line hidden until you've written or recorded it. A word is asked at most three times a lesson; padding cards say review, not again; warm-ups skip words typed today. Quick chat: known replies start at typing, its choices use your name, and the instruction is a tap away once you know it. Also: letters typed while their message is coming start your reply; New word cards say New word in the counter; placement results drop the 3 of 4; the bar keeps its place after a reload; Tricky words opens when it says it has words; Falling words fall in three lanes and say why a late answer didn't count; Today's conversation card says what's due; the shop says how close today's free rest day is; rating yourself has no answer picked for you; mano and tavo are stressed on the end (mah-NOH, tah-VOH); Prašau's card says "you're welcome" once; Aš is "I (as in I am)"; your name in Built from says (your name).
