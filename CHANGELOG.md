# Gintaras version history

- **3.122** (2026-11-12) League nicknames stay decent: rude, hurtful or fake-staff nicknames are refused in English, Lithuanian and Spanish (a refused first name falls back to "Learner" and a number). Tap a player in the league to challenge them or report their nickname; the owner can reset a reported nickname from Problem reports.

- **3.121** (2026-11-12) Problem reports can't be flooded: limits per internet address, per account and overall, with anything over a limit sent later. The privacy page explains the one-hour scrambled address, and reports that have been dealt with are deleted after 90 days.

- **3.120** (2026-11-12) Data safety. Starting a language again from zero now keeps your About me, face and settings, and your other devices no longer bring the old progress back. Signing in says up front what the league shows publicly. Signing out asks whether this is your own device and never clears one that holds unsaved progress. Deleting an account lists everything it removes, including the 30-day backups. The privacy page is rewritten to match what the app really stores. Old database setup scripts are locked, and every release checks that learners' progress is not reset.

- **3.119** (2026-11-09) Completed all 488 Lithuanian course picture mappings in the approved illustration style, with all 20 conversation portraits and the default learner. Existing number/day text tiles, vocabulary, recordings, progress IDs and custom avatars remain. Unit test-out keeps source phrases taught; recorded audio waits for the full file before seeking so cold-cache listening works reliably.

- **3.116** (2026-11-09) Required practical lessons now sit inside Lithuanian Units 1–3 and count toward unit completion. Vocabulary test-out defers skip credit until independent reading/listening/writing checks and recorded speaking are complete. Resume, offline access and previously learned words are preserved. Speaking is self-compared, not assigned a proficiency score.

- **3.115** (2026-11-08) Added 149 reviewed Lithuanian illustrations, bringing course picture coverage to 297 of 488. Existing pictures remain for the 191 pending replacements. Lesson content, IDs, recordings, activities, saved progress and persistent audio/picture caches are preserved.

- **3.114** (2026-11-08) Practical activities for the first three Lithuanian path units: Basics, Small talk and Food. Each has two reading tasks, two listening tasks, two writing tasks and a recording/comparison speaking task. Activities use exact previously taught course cards, unlock at their source lessons, offer optional hints, save locally and resume offline. All 99 illustrated cards in these units have the approved artwork. Existing lesson IDs, course order, audio, FSRS records and account sync are preserved. Later course development and Spanish remain outside this release. Deployment now packages only public site assets.

- **3.113** (2026-11-06) The current course flag opens a language chooser from the top bar, replacing the Settings selector. Switching preserves each course’s saved progress, updates direct preview links, and keeps unfinished languages behind Coming soon. Flags are cached for offline use; the chooser supports keyboard dismissal and mobile layouts.

- **3.112** (2026-11-05) First Lithuanian artwork rollout: 148 course pictures, all 20 conversation portraits and the default learner portrait use the approved rounded cartoon style. Course content, recordings, saved progress and customized avatars are preserved.

- **3.111** (2026-11-05) Gintaras is now Wordami. Ami the parrot replaces the amber-gem mascot with eight individually illustrated interaction poses; the app identity, installation, sharing, privacy copy and notification branding are updated. How the numbers work: the **first** number goes up for a big change, the **middle** number for a new feature, the **last** number for a fix or polish.

The app shows its version on the opening screen and at the bottom of Settings (`APP_RELEASE` in index.html).
